## Privacy Policy Template

### Sway Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** [DATE]

This Privacy Policy describes how Sway ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application and services (the "Service").

#### 1. Information We Collect

**Account Information**
- Anonymous user ID (generated automatically)
- Username (optional, chosen by you)
- Connection metadata (who you're connected with)

**Content You Create**
- Posts (text and images)
- Messages to your connections
- Reports you submit

**Usage Information**
- Device information (IP address, device type, browser)
- Usage patterns (features used, time spent)
- Error logs and performance data

**Images**
- Images you upload are stored on Cloudinary
- Images are associated with your user ID
- Image URLs may contain metadata

#### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain the Service
- Facilitate connections and messaging
- Moderate content and enforce Community Guidelines
- Improve the Service and develop new features
- Respond to reports and safety concerns
- Comply with legal obligations

#### 3. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information:
- **With Other Users**: Your username and posts are visible to other users. Messages are visible only to conversation participants.
- **With Service Providers**: Supabase (database), Cloudinary (image hosting), Cloudflare (infrastructure)
- **For Legal Reasons**: If required by law or to protect rights and safety
- **In Business Transfers**: If Sway is acquired or merged

We **never** sell your personal information.

#### 4. Data Retention

- Posts: Retained until you delete them
- Messages: Retained until you or your connection deletes them
- Account: Retained while your account is active
- Deleted content may be retained in backups for up to 90 days

#### 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your data
- Delete your account and data
- Opt out of optional data collection
- File a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

#### 6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us immediately.

#### 7. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, including:
- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
- Row-level security in our database

However, no system is 100% secure. Use the Service at your own risk.

#### 8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

#### 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of material changes by posting a notice in the app or sending you an email.

## Community Guidelines Template

### Sway Community Guidelines

Sway is a space for authentic expression and meaningful connections. To keep it safe and welcoming, we ask everyone to follow these guidelines.

#### What's Welcome on Sway

✅ **Authentic Expression**
- Share your thoughts, experiences, and feelings
- Ask questions and seek support
- Celebrate successes and navigate challenges

✅ **Respectful Disagreement**
- Disagree with ideas, not people
- Use "I" statements
- Assume good intent

✅ **Support and Kindness**
- Offer encouragement
- Listen without judgment
- Report content that concerns you

#### What's Not Allowed

❌ **Hate Speech**
- Attacks based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics
- Slurs or derogatory language
- Symbols or imagery associated with hate groups

❌ **Harassment and Bullying**
- Targeted harassment of individuals
- Repeated unwanted contact
- Threats or intimidation
- Doxxing (sharing personal information)
- Encouraging self-harm

❌ **Sexual Content**
- Sexually explicit content
- Sexual solicitation
- Sharing intimate images without consent
- Content sexualizing minors (zero tolerance)

❌ **Violence**
- Threats of violence
- Glorification of violence
- Graphic violent content
- Instructions for causing harm

❌ **Illegal Activity**
- Promotion of illegal activities
- Sale of illegal goods
- Instructions for illegal activities

❌ **Spam and Manipulation**
- Repetitive posts or messages
- Misleading information
- Impersonation
- Fake engagement

❌ **Self-Harm**
- Instructions or encouragement of self-harm
- Graphic self-harm content

*Note: Seeking support for mental health struggles is allowed and encouraged. If you're in crisis, please contact: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline*

#### Enforcement

**First Violation**: Warning and content removal  
**Second Violation**: Temporary suspension (24-72 hours)  
**Third Violation**: Permanent ban  
**Severe Violations**: Immediate permanent ban

Severe violations include:
- Content involving minors
- Credible threats of violence
- Doxxing
- Hate speech

#### Appeal Process

If you believe a moderation decision was made in error:
1. Email [APPEALS EMAIL] within 7 days
2. Include your username and explanation
3. We'll review and respond within 3-5 business days

#### Reporting

If you see content that violates these guidelines:
1. Tap the "Report" button on the content
2. Select the violation type
3. Provide additional context (optional)

All reports are reviewed by our moderation team.

#### Questions?

Contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL]

## Data Retention Policy

### Sway Data Retention Policy

This policy describes how long we retain different types of data.

#### Active Accounts

While your account is active, we retain:
- All your posts (until you delete them)
- All your messages (until you or your connection deletes them)
- Your username and profile information
- Your connection list

#### Deleted Content

When you delete content:
- It is removed from the app immediately
- It may remain in backups for up to 90 days
- It may be retained longer if needed for legal reasons or safety investigations

#### Deleted Accounts

When you delete your account:
- Your profile is deleted immediately
- Your posts and messages are deleted within 30 days
- Some data (reports, moderation records) may be retained for legal compliance
- Backup copies are deleted within 90 days

#### Legal Compliance

We may retain data longer if:
- Required by law
- Necessary for ongoing safety investigations
- Subject to legal hold or court order

#### Anonymized Data

We may retain anonymized data indefinitely for:
- Analytics and improvements
- Research purposes
- Aggregate statistics
